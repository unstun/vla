## 1. v47–v85 (Oct 2–5): pushing the custom SCAN local planner toward 30 Hz

- v47 reuses the feasible window. First-seed search failures fall from 73.69% to 0.22%; local planning runs at 16.232 Hz on gpu5070ti. `versions/v47/RESULTS.md`.
- v48 warm-starts from the owned remaining curve, with fallback to the original graph. Median solve 3.092 ms, P99 207.175 ms, local 15.859 Hz. It stayed the runnable experimental reference for this whole lineage. `versions/v48/RESULTS.md`.
- v49 adds a shared 20 ms compute / 25 ms total budget. It was rejected and excluded from ancestry because the backend ignores its new timeout reason (踩坑 §4).
- v50, a child of v48, adds a necessary-condition precheck that skips futile final-goal searches. Paired replay max falls from 107.481 to 4.827 ms, but live maxima remain 1829.626 and 858.031 ms.
- v52 scopes a terrain-cell lookup cache. Idle paired max falls from 473.514 to 422.981 ms; the live run reaches 10.19 Hz successful planning with a 711.404 ms maximum. `versions/v52/RESULTS.md`.
- v53–v57 target corner continuity: compatible route refresh (v53), a warm-binding clock guard (v54), DDS drain at prefix admission (v55), a typed physical-floor snapshot (v56), and metadata only (v57). v57 passes the old corner without a pause over 1 s, at 25.65/25.76 Hz with maxima of 660.975/290.149 ms. `versions/v57/RESULTS.md`.
- v58 adds an endpoint-evidence certificate (goal-search max 44.614 → 0.259 ms). v59 gives trajectories a shared time origin. Their recordings reach 27.53/26.43 Hz successful planning, with maxima of 659.526/1236.709 ms.
- v59's retry-epoch defect (踩坑 §3) was fixed in two steps, both tested only as Mac components: v62 carries the timestamp through the retry chain, and v63 makes rebound repair return failure after a failed initialization.
- v64–v68 are Mac host packages with no planner change; v70 adds observation-only tracing.
- v71–v77 probe the pinned upstream ROS 2 planning core. v72 produced 0 moving plans; v77 had 10/1226 successful chains, then stalled. The upstream core was not adopted.
- v79–v82 rework mapping: compact `/state` polling (v79; encode mean 42.428 → 0.307 ms); equivalent semantic membership and inflation addressing (v80); single-writer immutable map snapshots with a publication lease (v81); guarded duplicate-backlog reduction (v82).
- The v82 Mac trials ran at 11.114/10.959 Hz with 23.8/31.3 s pauses and failed. `audits/2026-10-05_safe_mapping_repair/REPORT.md`.
- v84 replays reject plain latest-only scan admission: under two 650 ms stalls it drops 12 scans and loses 173 occupied cells. v85, a three-thread prototype, was tested at component level only.
- v51 made streaming MP4 plus managed retention the default artifact pipeline.
- Outcome: sustained 30 Hz was never met.

## 2. v86–v121 (Oct 8–9): migration to actual ROS 2 Foxy Nav2

- v86–v91 build the Nav2 stack: Foxy controller and goal plugins over an owned native SCAN executor (v86); a Nav2-style temporal observation buffer (v87); stock Nav2 goal/cancel on a flat kinematic model (v88); the PCT GlobalPlanner plugin, a shared native-map TerrainLayer, a mission gateway, and BT binding/stop nodes (v89); native PCT built for ARM64 / Python 3.8 (v90); 20 Hz mapper scheduling (v91).
- v92–v99 are bring-up repairs: ARM64 ZeroMQ, isolated NumPy 1.24.4, sensor compile before timed sensing, an explicit 1000 ms BT action-ack timeout, a cancel-acknowledgment fix, and canonical integer ROS time. Causes are in 踩坑 §5. `audits/2026-10-08_nav2_full/REPORT.zh.md`.
- v100–v113 repair runs in the original scene; the 100/250 ms age limits are unchanged.
  - Timing and lifecycle: a separate Foxy clock lane (v100); body messages admitted only when due (v101); a checked old curve renewed during replanning (v102); superseded output tickets discarded (v103); owned executors stopped and joined on cancel-before-spin (v104).
  - Proofs and mapping: spline/proof pairs activated atomically (v107); a 100 Hz mapper cap (v109); the final whole-curve proof published with its spline (v110); equivalent inflation columns, about 43% less mapping CPU (v113).
- v114 lets the controller run its own native check of the full remaining curve, using a pinned immutable map revision, its own tracker phase and its own body/clock. Result: the first Nav2 arrival in the original scene, from the corner start, on Mac. Numbers: 26.551 m, RTF 0.999965, two protected retries, 17.984 Hz over the whole window, max round 128.411 ms. `audits/2026-10-08_nav2_sim_repair/REPORT.md`.
- v115–v121 are display and timing only, including a 900-round timing HTML (`audits/2026-10-09_local_curve_timing/REPORT.md`).
