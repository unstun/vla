# Isaac Runner PD Stance Hold For V12 Stops

Archived at 2026-09-17 21:15 America/Los_Angeles. Stage: experiment + analysis.
Task: `09-17-isaac-runner-stance-hold`, still `in_progress`. All Isaac runs are
on gpu5070ti; analysis is local. Scope is the VLA Isaac runner only; the
`machine-dog` deployment program is unchanged (PRD D1). Paths below are relative
to `.pipeline/experiments/2026-09-16_pi_cortex_harnessvln/` unless shown in full.

## Changes And Evidence

- **Stance-hold state machine added to the Isaac runner (commit `eae4a817`).**
  `isaac_runner/stance_hold.py` ports the `machine-dog` MuJoCo stand state
  machine: RL → STAND_SETTLING (settle gates) → STAND_TRANSITION (2 s) →
  FIXED_STAND (Kp 60 / Kd 1) → REJOIN (0.25 s smoothstep back to RL). It is
  inserted through `materialize_runner_patch.py` as `--stance-hold {off,auto}`;
  the runner patch default stays `off`. Run C1 (`stand_20260917T125239Z`, step
  entry) passed AC3 over 55.7 s of FIXED_STAND: |yaw rate| ≤ 0.0038 rad/s, drift
  2.5 mm, against 0.143 rad/s and 3.4 cm with the hold off. It hopped at entry
  (+0.21 m/s vertical, 0.30 rad/s yaw); see the pitfall record of the same date.
- **Entry law iterations.** D4 (`8c245bc0`): 0.5 s gain ramp at entry and AC7
  added; the hop disappeared but yaw reached 0.363 rad/s (C1b). D5
  (`35868efb`): scripted walk-stop-start test, side camera, AC8 and entry-Kd
  variants `ramp` / `step1` / `step2p5`; none passed AC8 b. After a survey of
  published RL → PD switches
  ([survey](../../../.pipeline/survey/2026-09-17_rl_pd_stance_hold_switch_survey.md)),
  D6 (`02e4e55b`) added the crossfade entries `xfade1s` / `xfade2s`: the entry
  step changes only Kd (to 1.0) and applies V12's live PD law, then blends it
  into the hold law over the crossfade time, after a 1.0 s landing grace. The
  walk runs (`a826eb75`, patched runner `0649747e…`) kept every entry at or
  below 0.087 rad/s; the D5 rule selected `xfade1s`.
- **Two-goal series C2 (commit `87f3c0d1`).** Eight `scan_04` episodes with
  `--stance-hold auto` and `xfade1s` passed the unchanged 41 checks, 8/8
  (one-sided 95 % Clopper-Pearson lower bound 0.688; the hold-off SCAN series was
  7/8, 0.529). Ledger: `stance_hold_qualification/hold_series/series_ledger.jsonl`.
  New tooling: `run_hold_series.py` (series driver), `recover_interrupted_episode.py`
  (collects and reviews an episode whose remote worker finished after the local
  driver died, using the launcher's own `collect_remote_only`; remote `passed`
  not true → `execute_failed`; executed copy
  `recover_interrupted_episode.executed_10993936.py` kept), `series_report.py`
  (AC4 report, Clopper-Pearson bound, still windows) and
  `make_result_figures.py` (five figures). The 8/8 pass is not evidence that the
  hold keeps the robot still in the review windows: all 24 still-window peaks
  lie in STAND_SETTLING, and no two-goal stop reached FIXED_STAND.
- **D7, post-result AC7 amendment (Dr Sun, 2026-09-18 UTC; commit `fe82a471`).**
  The AC7 entry window now ends at the restart, the first row after entry whose
  mode is neither STAND_TRANSITION nor FIXED_STAND (excluded). Thresholds were
  re-frozen as `34c15fe6…` with an `amendment` record; `0be59927…` is archived in
  `isaac_runner/stance_hold_thresholds_history/`. No limit changed.
  `analyze_stance_hold.py` reports the amended verdict and
  `as_frozen_before_results` side by side; AC8 b and the D5 selection keep the
  full window. The only verdict change is two-goal episode 6: amended pass
  0.063 rad/s, as frozen fail 0.174 rad/s (restart at 6.84 s, step 341, 0.18 s
  after a calm entry at 6.66 s).
- **D8, launcher default (commit `fe82a471`).** `isaac_runner/qualify_stand_runner.py`
  and `isaac_runner/run_two_goal_pilot.py` default to `--stance-hold auto`; the
