## What was reached

The VLA Isaac runner can hand the joints from V12 (`model_149999.pt`) to a PD stance hold on a zero command and
hand them back when a command arrives. The selected entry is `xfade1s`: V12's live PD command crossfades into the
hold over 1 s. All runs were in Isaac Lab on gpu5070ti.
