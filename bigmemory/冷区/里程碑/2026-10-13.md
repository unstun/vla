## 3. v177: 5/5 original-route repetition on Linux (2026-10-12)

**Claim.** On gpu5070ti, with the unchanged original Building map and the
original spatial start, five serial runs all arrived:

- paths of 76.846, 76.946, 77.097, 76.871 and 76.910 m;
- 30 qualified measured-stop handoffs and no falls;
- every run passed the final XY/Z/yaw, PD hold and copyback checks;
- all 55 compact return files were SHA-verified.

The only behaviour change from v175 is the local spline finish radius, from
15 cm to 2 cm. Label: `validated` for the declared original-map completion and
recovery condition only.

**Evidence.** `versions/v177/RESULTS.md`,
`versions/v177/results/cohort_summary.json`, and the v178 review video
(`versions/v178/delivered/v177_full_route_attempt01.mp4`).

**Boundary.**

- The longest low-speed pause is 10.694 s and planning peaks reach 497.846 ms.
  This is not smoothness or 30 Hz acceptance.
- Attempt01 drifted about 0.957 m under zero command while in STAND_SETTLING.
  Stopped-pose retention is not validated.
- A later separate cohort of the existing v177 binary (attempts 06–10) also
  arrived 5/5. However, the unchanged-source rebuild v188 arrived only 3/5. So
  5/5 is not universal stability, and cohorts must not be pooled.
- No other map, Jetson, robot or broad statistical reliability claim is made.
