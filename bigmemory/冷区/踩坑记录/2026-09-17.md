## Pitfall 1: A Series Driver Started As A Claude Background Task Dies With The Session

### Symptom

The two-goal series ledger had episodes 1–2 only. Episode 3
(`two_goal_20260917T184758Z_hold_e03`) had no collected files.

### Verified Root Cause

The local driver process was gone with the ended session, while the remote
worker had written a final, passed `result.json` at 2026-09-17T18:50:24Z. The
remote worker's SSH stdout log (`02_remote_worker.log`) was lost with the killed
SSH session.

### Fix

Start long drivers detached (`nohup … &`) and follow the ledger with a monitor.
A finished remote episode is collected, never re-run (re-running would drop a
finished outcome): `stance_hold_qualification/recover_interrupted_episode.py`
uses the launcher's own `collect_remote_only`, requires the final result phase,
treats remote `passed` not true as `execute_failed`, and marks the ledger line
`recovered_after_driver_interrupt` (collected 2026-09-18T02:58:17Z). Episodes
4–8 ran from `--from-episode 4`.

## Pitfall 2: Gain Step Against Delayed Position Targets Makes The Body Hop

### Symptom

Run C1 (`isaac_runner/runs/stand_20260917T125239Z_hold_auto`) stood still after
entry but hopped at the hold entry: +0.21 m/s vertical speed, 0.30 rad/s yaw.

### Verified Root Cause

V12's `Lite3Actuator` (subclass of `DelayedPDActuator`) applies position targets
late (recorded lag 3 physics substeps, range 0–4) while stiffness and damping act
at once. The immediate Kp 20 → 60 step therefore met the previous target; the
row estimate `delayed_target_torque_max_ratio_estimate` rose from 0.16 to 0.49.
The MuJoCo source design has no actuator delay. Evidence: the runner's
`stance_hold_setup.json` (`position_delay_time_lags`, `delayed_target_note`) and
`isaac_runner/README.md` "Delayed targets". A 0.5 s gain ramp (C1b) removed the
hop but swung yaw to 0.363 rad/s; entry-Kd steps (C1d) failed AC8 b.

### Fix

Crossfade entry `xfade1s` (PRD D6, commit `02e4e55b`): keep V12's own PD law and
gains at the switch step (only Kd to 1.0), then blend into the hold law over 1 s.
Walk entries stayed at or below 0.087 rad/s. Lesson: before switching controllers
in Isaac, read the actuator class for delayed targets versus immediate gains.

## Pitfall 3: A Fixed Post-Switch Window Also Scores The Next Event

### Symptom

Two-goal episode 6 failed AC7 as frozen (0.174 rad/s against 0.15) after a calm
hold entry.

### Verified Root Cause

The frozen AC7 window ran a fixed 1.0 s after entry. The planner resumed goal A
0.18 s after the entry at 6.66 s (restart row 6.84 s, step 341, REJOIN), and the
normal V12 restart peak at 6.92 s fell inside the window. Up to the restart, the
window maximum was 0.063 rad/s (`series_d7_20260918.json`).

### Fix

Dr Sun's post-result amendment D7 ends the window at the restart; both verdicts
are reported and the evidence label stays `integrated`. Lesson: define an end
condition (mode change, new command) for every post-switch window when the
criterion is frozen, not after a failure.

## Pitfall 4: The Review Stop Check Does Not Measure The Hold

### Symptom

Hold-on and hold-off two-goal series had the same stop-check margin (median
still-window max |yaw rate| 0.120 vs 0.121 rad/s), despite an 8/8 versus 7/8
series result.

### Verified Root Cause

The review still window (1 s before each terminal) runs 0.10–1.40 s after the
zero command. There V12 still drives the joints in STAND_SETTLING while the hold
waits for its gates; all 24 still-window peaks fall in STAND_SETTLING and no
two-goal stop reached FIXED_STAND.

### Fix

Before crediting a controller change for a stop-dwell pass, locate each window
peak in the mode timeline (`series_report.py` → `still_windows`,
`zero_command_intervals`). An earlier entry or longer dwell is a separate
decision.

## Pitfall 5: Subsampled Rows And Assumed Joint Order Corrupt Event Analysis

### Symptom

The first analysis of episode 6 reported the restart at 6.86 s, 0.20 s after
entry, and an entry maximum of 0.053 rad/s. The 0.20 s value is in the commit
message of `87f3c0d1`. Separately, a per-leg joint-order assumption was wrong.

### Verified Root Cause

Only even control steps were printed. The first REJOIN row is odd step 341
(6.84 s, 0.18 s after entry), and the amended-window maximum 0.063 rad/s is at odd
step 335. The metrics joint order is the robot articulation order, grouped by
joint type (HipX×4, HipY×4, Knee×4), not per leg; the V12 action term maps to it
through `_joint_ids` (`stance_hold_setup.json`).

### Fix

The corrected values are in `RESULT.md` and commit `fe82a471`. Lesson: never
subsample rows when locating an event, and read `stance_hold_setup.json` for joint
order before per-joint analysis.
