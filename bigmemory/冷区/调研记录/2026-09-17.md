## Findings

- **No direct precedent.** No surveyed stack or paper switches automatically from an RL policy to a joint-PD hold
  on a zero command while changing PD gains during the switch. No source reports a numeric smoothness measurement
  for such a switch (survey, bottom line; code note, "Not found").
- **Keep the gains, move only the target over about 1–2 s:**
  - `fan-ziqi/rl_sar@376d42c` Lite3: key-triggered mid-walk stand, linear 1.0 s from measured q
    (`fsm_lite3.hpp:86-91,204-208`). `himloco/config.yaml` sets fixed_kp/kd = rl_kp/kd = 40/1 [verified].
  - LimX `tron1-rl-deploy-ros@14ac8d1`: 1.0 s at the walking gains 42/3.5. The code note tags it [verified]; the
    survey table tags it [agent-verified].
  - walk-these-ways: target rate-limited to 0.05 rad per 20 Hz step, policy gains unchanged.
- **Start from the measured state:** `DeepRoboticsLab/Lite3_rl_deploy@1c4a8f1` uses a cubic from measured q and dq
  with gains stepped to 100/2.5 (`liedown_state.hpp:98-112`) [verified]. This is a manual lie-down, not a
  zero-command hold.
- **Switch at a suitable moment:** Disney biped (arXiv 2501.05204 §VII-A) delays the switch to the next double
  support [verified]. Safe-Stop (arXiv 2609.02358 §4.3) requires both estimators to pass on each of the last K
  samples [verified].
- **Stand inside RL:** GR00T WBC `@7f15131` selects a separately trained Balance policy at |cmd| < 0.05, with no
  dwell or blend (`g1_gear_wbc_policy.py:221-230`) [verified]. Safe-Stop's stop policy commands the frozen
  locomotion network (§5.1) [verified]. These need training and are out of scope for this task.
- **Damping from a damping ratio:** BeyondMimic G1 uses ωn = 10 Hz and ζ = 2 (`robots/g1.py:7-23` @cd65172)
  [verified]. The project's own estimate for V12 (Kp 20 / Kd 0.5) is ζ ≈ 0.2 (theory note, worked numbers).
- **Theory:**
  - Torque continuity at the switch is only half the problem. The torque-matched seed is Hanus et al. 1987's
    "realizable reference" [lead]. Cheong & Safonov 2008 also require no fast transient after the switch
    [verified]. Raise damping before stiffness.
  - The Kronander & Billard 2016 condition was read through Liang et al. 2022 and re-derived [verified-2nd]. By the
    project's calculation, the 0.5 s ramp passes it for H < 0.28 kg·m², so the ramp shape is not the limit.
- **Simulator fact:** Isaac Lab `DelayedPDActuator` delays position, velocity and effort targets, but applies the
  current stiffness and damping at once (`actuator_pd.py:359-367`, main `b0542fe`) [verified]. In the V12 runner a
  gain change therefore leads its target by 3 substeps (15 ms). The Lite3 MotionSDK (`@b30a3ec`,
  `include/robot_types.h:59-79`) carries kp, kd, target and torque in one per-joint command [agent-verified; the
  send path is a prebuilt `.so`]. No surveyed code compensates for such a lag.
- **Check against the project's own C1d walk runs** (`isaac_runner/runs/walk_20260917T145650Z_*`, gpu5070ti, no
  new run):
  - the body was already quiet before every entry (angular speed ≤ 0.17 rad/s), and the kick began 0.04–0.08 s
    after entry, so a stricter body gate is unlikely to help;
  - V12 holds knee targets 0.24–0.31 rad from the measured knee angle at entry;
  - damping at entry reduced the kick at stops.
- **Untested project bound:** knee Kd ≲ 1.45 at a 5 ms explicit step with zero armature (theory note, item 6). The
  50 Hz logs cannot test it.

## Mechanisms listed but not validated for V12 (at survey time)

These were candidates: a crossfade from V12's live target, per-group damping from a damping ratio, delaying gain
changes by the actuator lag, and a spawn-landing grace. The crossfade and the 1.0 s landing grace became PRD D6.
Only the crossfade was then qualified (see `心路历程/2026-09-17.md`).
