# Upstream surveys behind the Nav2 migration, recovery repair and stopping work

Archived on 2026-10-13 07:05 America/Los_Angeles. Stage: experiment + analysis.

2026-10-13 under `.pipeline/survey/`. Every repository named here has the

The archive writer re-read the six survey files but did not fetch upstream

## 1. Mapping backlog, 2026-10-08

Source: `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-08_mapping_backlog_upstream_survey.md`.
Trigger: the failed v82 continuity trials, with queues of up to six clouds,
background batches of 816/1,062 ms and 11.1/11.0 Hz useful planning.

- Eight repositories: Nav2, ROG-Map, SUPER, Elevation Mapping CuPy, nvblox,
  EGO-Planner, SCAN-Planner and PCT-SCAN-ROS2.
- The inspected systems bound admission: a latest-cloud slot, a bounded queue
  that drops the oldest item, or a minimum integration interval. They also run
  mapping, trajectory and control at separate rates. None of them keeps every
  observation under overload. v82's contract (fuse every distinct scan, and
  reject map evidence older than 250 ms) is stricter than all of them.
- Our own upstream differs from us. The pinned PCT-SCAN-ROS2 LiDAR callback
  sets a dirty flag and a 50 ms timer fuses the cloud
  ([dirty-gated fusion](https://github.com/yagami-light7/PCT-SCAN-ROS2/blob/1cf45a1c12ca785ce533293302be09d51daf0ccb/src/planner/plan_env/src/grid_map.cpp#L743-L772)).
  Local v50 fuses inside the callback instead. The commit that introduced this
  difference is not established.
- ROG-Map keeps one cloud slot, and a later arrival overwrites it
  ([callbacks](https://github.com/hku-mars/ROG-Map/blob/df59c21304579a13fb3875100f8ce9523ba379a0/rog_map/src/rog_map/rog_map.cpp#L285-L330)).
  Admitting only the latest cloud can lose a transient obstacle.
- SUPER keeps the committed trajectory and builds a backup before it replaces
  the trajectory. This addresses behaviour during a delay, not mapper
  throughput.
- What followed: v84 replayed the same scans under FIFO, timer and latest-only
  admission. Latest-only lost 173 originally occupied cells under injected
  stalls and was rejected. v85 was a component prototype, and v87 adopted a
  Nav2-style 0.5 s observation window.

## 2. Local-planning failures, 2026-10-09

Source: `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-09_local_planning_failure_upstream_survey.md`.

- Comparable planners anticipate failed local plans in their code:
  - SCAN tries three initializations, and a failure counter leads to an
    emergency stop
    ([FSM](https://github.com/wuyi2121/SCAN-Planner/blob/348e8a590a50a5a6bbab8d8c6dcfd171f009be26/src/planner/plan_manage/src/scan_replan_fsm.cpp#L687)).
  - EGO also has three initialization choices.
  - TEB sends a zero command, resets and can use a shorter horizon.
  - Nav2 Foxy 0.4.7 catches `PlannerException`, publishes zero velocity and
    ends FollowPath.
- The public issue reports (TEB #7, EGO-Planner-v2 #56, linorobot2 #139) are
  the reporters' claims and were not reproduced [未验证].
- Three failure types must be kept apart: graph-search failure, optimizer
  failure, and a curve rejected by a later check. In our recording, the two slow
  failed rounds failed before optimization.

## 3. Navigation qualification, 2026-10-09

Sources: `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-09_navigation_qualification_upstream_survey.md`,
its collection scripts in `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-09_navigation_qualification_sources/`,
and the inventory, comparison and hash records in
`references/upstream/2026-10-09_navigation_qualification/`.

- The survey covered 18 repositories (15 implementations or frameworks and 3
  benchmarks) and records hash checks for 63 selected files [未验证]. Several
  share lineage (SCAN, EGO and EGO-v2), so they are not 18 independent
  baselines.
- Conclusion: no published practice disqualifies a system because one local
  solve fails, one round exceeds 60 ms, or updates fall below 30 Hz. Published
  evaluations judge whole tasks under a declared protocol. Examples:
  - SCAN uses 50 trials per scene with a 120 s timeout
    ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19555v1#S6)).
  - ArtPlanner targets 0.5 Hz ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.01420)).
  - In FlightBench, EGO succeeds 20% of the time in the hard maze despite
    millisecond planning
    ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.05687v3#S4)).
  - Arena-Bench counts fewer than two collisions as a success.
- The survey withdrew an earlier blanket "unqualified" verdict as too strong.
  The project's own sustained 30 Hz acceptance still stands and is still unmet.
- Our v116 attempt02 had 895/900 successful rounds (median 1.580 ms, P99
  17.652 ms, maximum 138.782 ms), yet the mission FAILED. The share of
  successful rounds is not a mission success rate. A single v114 arrival
  together with one failure must not be published as "50%".
- FASTER keeps the previous committed trajectory, which includes a safe stop,
  when replanning is infeasible
  ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.04420#S3)). This is an architecture
  reference only.
- The recommended next step was repeated complete trials over declared
  scenarios. The later 6-per-arm, 3-pilot and 5-trial cohort designs follow
  this recommendation (inference by this writer).

## 4. v177 stop and recovery, 2026-10-12

Source: `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-12_v177_stop_recovery_upstream_survey.md`.
Stop audit: `audits/2026-10-12_v177_stop_causes/` (27 source/license files from
10 repositories, hashes matched fresh downloads [未验证]). Three locomotion
references are in `references/upstream/2026-10-12_stop_hold/`. The survey
covers 13 repositories in total.

- Stopping the output, retrying the computation and abandoning the route are
  separate actions. Current Nav2 retries inside MPPI and gives the controller
  failure patience
  ([Nav2 bcc9c48](https://github.com/ros-navigation/navigation2/tree/bcc9c48cf468588559d01942b72d511a6b990891)).
  Foxy 0.4.7 does not provide this by default.
- Repeated failure should change the search problem. TEB shortens its horizon.
  The CMU Go2 stack reduces path scale and range, and publishes a one-point stop
  path when no path remains
  ([CMU Go2](https://github.com/jizhang-cmu/autonomy_stack_go2/tree/43d5f54b389b251713f0097893c30fa76c870d54)).
- Failure categories must stay visible. Our label `scan_safety_evidence_stale`
  mixes geometric rejection with data age.
- v177 attempt01 moved about 0.957 m under zero command while still in
  STAND_SETTLING. A completed route therefore does not validate stopped-pose
  retention.
- The official Lite3 `sdk_deploy` stays in RL control when velocity is zero; it
  does not switch to stand
  ([sdk_deploy](https://github.com/DeepRoboticsLab/sdk_deploy/tree/9ea6ee94bfe90c9a36f5799d39f8dc1d821128db)).
- Proposed order (not a decision):
  1. Qualify stopped-pose retention.
  2. Separate rejection reasons.
  3. Add a bounded wait for transient data.
  4. Prove that the recovery heading is feasible.
  5. Only then consider near-term safe continuation.

## 5. RL zero-command standing, 2026-10-12 (corrected 2026-10-13)

Source: `.pipeline/survey/2026-10-12_rl_zero_command_upstream_survey.md`.
Manifest: `references/upstream/2026-10-12_rl_zero_command/repository_manifest.yaml`.

- Standing still at zero command is a locomotion capability that comes from
  command sampling and reward terms. The seven repositories differ:
  - robot_lab enables `stand_still=-2` and zeroes horizontal commands at or
    below 0.2 m/s
    ([robot_lab](https://github.com/fan-ziqi/robot_lab/tree/500399ed75f510aeaff28705a8ce736c514dbec3)).
  - The legged_gym base default is `stand_still=-0.0`
    ([legged_gym](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/legged_gym/tree/8fa29acc6fd1910c3d9659eef6310bdd301cde0a)).
  - unitree_rl_mjlab masks `feet_slip` at zero command.
  - In Walk These Ways, the gait frequency is independent of the velocity
    command.
- Our V12 source was read with `git show` at machine-dog
  `8c3fdffa84b85be0704a10ea5b2533817d543822`; the sibling working tree was
  not touched. Findings:
  - Its "zero command" branch clears only the x and y commands, not yaw.
  - `stand_still` is not connected.
  - The actor observation has no world-space parking target.
  These are investigation leads, not proof of the cause of the v177 drift.
- Correction: the per-leg handoff and the 2 cm / 10 s hold target were
  unvalidated hypotheses.
- Speed correction from Dr Sun, 2026-10-13: v179 used 0.2/0.35/0.5 m/s, below
  the project's 0.6 m/s minimum planned cruise. v182 used 0.6/0.8/1.0 m/s:
  - Flat ground: late excursions over 5 cm fell from 4/12 to 0/12, and the
    maximum from 31.15 cm to 3.67 cm.
  - Stairs: 4/12 flags remain, with a maximum of 10.08 cm.
  No standing controller, model or training change was selected.

## Cross-cutting implications

- Upstream code shows that failure is anticipated. It gives no upstream
  failure rates and gives no licence to relax freshness, collision or support
  gates.
- Current-main Nav2 behaviour (MPPI retries, typed failure patience) must not
  be assumed in the pinned Foxy 0.4.7.
- Navigation evidence and locomotion evidence stay separate. A completed route
  is not a zero-command standing result.
