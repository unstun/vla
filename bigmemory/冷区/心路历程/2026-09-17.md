# Isaac runner stance hold: decision path and reasons

Archived at 2026-09-17 21:15 America/Los_Angeles (2026-09-18 04:15 UTC). Stage: experiment + analysis.
Task: `09-17-isaac-runner-stance-hold` (parent `09-16-emos-harnessvln-reimplementation`). Sources:
`.trellis/tasks/09-17-isaac-runner-stance-hold/prd.md` (Decisions D1–D8) and
`.pipeline/experiments/2026-09-16_pi_cortex_harnessvln/stance_hold_qualification/RESULT.md`. The PRD dates D7 and
D8 as 2026-09-18, which is the UTC date. Both were made on 2026-09-17 America/Los_Angeles, after the C2 series
ended at 2026-09-18 03:14 UTC.

## Why the task exists

Dr Sun: RL gaits cannot stand still, so a real stop needs a smooth switch to a PD stance hold, and that switch is
hard. The trigger was SCAN-fix series episode 3, which failed goal-B stillness at 0.1595 against 0.15 rad/s while
V12 (`model_149999.pt`) ran on a zero command with no PD hold (PRD Background). The runner had no PD hold after
RL.

## Decisions and why (为什么)

- **D1, scope: the VLA Isaac runner only; `machine-dog` stays read-only.** Why: the failing evidence comes from the
  Isaac pilot. Sibling repositories stay read-only unless Dr Sun opens a task there (AGENTS.md), so PRD D1 leaves a
  deployment fix to a separate task in `machine-dog`.
- **D2, lead defaults: port the machine-dog MuJoCo state machine, Kp 60 / Kd 1, automatic trigger.** Why: Dr Sun
  declined the design questions and said "continue". The MuJoCo design was already qualified: 9/9
  move-stand-rejoin runs, and a 73 s hold drifted 1.1 mm.
- **D3, run budget approved** (C1 stand A/B, then C2 with 8 two-goal episodes, on gpu5070ti).
- **D4, 0.5 s gain ramp at entry, and AC7 added before the rerun.** Why: C1 passed AC3, but the entry hopped
  (+0.21 m/s vertical, 0.30 rad/s yaw). The immediate Kp 20 → 60 step acted at once, while V12's actuator applies
  position targets 3 substeps late. The ramp removed the hop but swung yaw to 0.363 rad/s (C1b).
- **D5, scripted walk-stop-start test with a side camera (AC8) and entry-damping variants.** Why: standing alone
  does not show whether the RL ↔ PD switch is smooth. The selection rule was fixed before the runs: among
  variants that pass AC5 and AC8, take the lowest entry-window max |yaw rate|. Result: every C1d variant (ramp,
  step1, step2p5) failed AC8 b at one or more entries.
- **Survey before another home-made design.** Dr Sun objected to designing the entry in isolation and asked for
  published practice from papers and GitHub. The survey found no source that changes PD gains at an RL → PD
  switch. Code keeps the gains and moves the target over 1–2 s (rl_sar Lite3, LimX), or switches only at a
  suitable moment (Disney biped, Safe-Stop). Theory adds bumpless transfer and damping before stiffness. The
  project's own C1d data showed that the body was already quiet before every entry. Details are in
  `调研记录/2026-09-17.md`.
- **D6, option 1: crossfade from V12's live PD command into the hold over 1 s, Kd 1.0 from entry, and a 1.0 s
  landing grace.** Why: this follows the surveyed practice of keeping the policy's law and moving smoothly, instead
  of stepping gains against a delayed target. `xfade1s` was chosen by the unchanged D5 rule: walk entries stayed at
  or below 0.087 rad/s, the yaw already present on each entry row. The landing grace was fixed before the runs as
  a design choice, not an exclusion.
- **D7, post-result: the AC7 window ends at a restart.** C2 passed 8/8 (lower bound 0.688; hold-off SCAN series
  7/8), but AC7 as frozen failed in episode 6. Why Dr Sun chose the amendment: the entry itself was calm
  (≤ 0.063 rad/s). The planner resumed 0.18 s later, and the 0.174 rad/s peak came from V12's normal restart inside
  REJOIN, not from an entry kick. The frozen 1.0 s window had no clause for a restart, and no earlier hold-on run
  had restarted within 1 s of an entry. Conditions attached: the change is labelled post-result, both verdicts are
  reported, and the evidence label stays `integrated`, not `validated`.
- **D8: `--stance-hold auto` (resolving to `xfade1s`) becomes the launcher default. The runner patch default stays
  `off`, and reproducing callers are pinned `off`.** Why: C2 ran with xfade1s. The old `ramp` failed AC7 in C1b,
  so auto with ramp would be an unqualified default. Keeping the runner default and its hashed bytes, reading old
  packages with their legacy meaning, and pinning the SCAN-fix driver and point-cloud probe to `off` keep earlier
  evidence reproducible.

## Finding that limits the claim

The hold does not change the two-goal stop check. The review's still window (the 1 s before each terminal) falls
in STAND_SETTLING, while V12 still drives. All 24 still-window peaks are there, with a median of 0.120 rad/s with the
hold and 0.121 without. No two-goal stop reached FIXED_STAND. What changed was stillness once the hold was
reached (walk-stop tail yaw ≤ 0.0017 rad/s against 0.065–0.117 with the hold off) and gentler restarts (median
0.165 against 0.201 rad/s; max 0.197 against 0.341). An earlier entry (looser gates) or a longer dwell before the
terminal would be a separate decision. None was taken.
