## Scope and remaining manual check

This validates one synthetic-audio-to-simulator-to-video path. It does not validate
Dr Sun's own speech or microphone capture. The MacBook Pro microphone was
enumerated successfully; `录音并生成视频.command` waits for Enter before recording
six seconds. First use may require macOS microphone permission.

The current offline demonstration accepts the vending-machine goal only. It does
not provide interactive stop/resume. The endpoint stop is the evaluator's distance
threshold, not a learned stopping action. Crowd-path/character warnings from the
upstream scene remain. This is not a paper reproduction or a Lite3 experiment.

The cyan trajectory is projected onto the camera image without depth occlusion,
so a line can appear over the robot/people. Orange is the recorded driven path.
Only the output image copy is annotated; the model sees original RGB pixels.

## Frozen first-call diagnosis

The subsequent [frozen-input diagnostic](frozen_replay_2026-09-08/RESULT.md)
reproduced both original first trajectories exactly. Sixteen real-model calls
crossed the two initial RGB frames with both languages and seeds 36/37/38,
including identical-input repeats. At fixed pixels/features/state/delay, Chinese
produced near-origin plans and strong desired right turns on both images;
English produced longer forward plans and small heading corrections. All eight
fixed-input cache exchanges reproduced the donor output exactly. The source
controller's near-origin lookahead geometry explains its strong angular request.
All 97 source/asset gates and 11 successful-run artifact hashes passed locally.
This isolates a first-call effect, not a unique neural root cause or a fix for
the full closed-loop Chinese failure. No model or simulator behavior was changed.
