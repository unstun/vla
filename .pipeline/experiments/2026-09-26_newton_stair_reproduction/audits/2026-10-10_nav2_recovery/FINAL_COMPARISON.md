final comparison trial. Six fresh unchanged v114 controls and six frozen v128
candidates use the same frozen v128 host, CPU Newton and original scene.
Order A2,B2,B3,A3 / B4,A4,A5,B5 / A6,B6,B7,A7 (A=baseline v114).
The previous three v114 diagnostic runs and candidate pilot are excluded from
primary six-per-arm results. No failure replacement or early stopping on mission
failure. Stop only on resource/source/execution/retention error; preserve it.

Primary operational evidence: arrival with measured hold, failures, falls,
negative-safety stop-to-FollowPath-abort delay, actual new-owner admission,
replacement trajectory and resumed movement. Predeclare next-tick notification
expectation as no unchanged ten-second progress wait; report actual delays and
any scheduling outliers, rather than masking them with a changed threshold.
All controller/body/map age, stop proof, native geometry and retries unchanged.
No reliability acceptance from one pilot or component test. Record whole-round
timing tails separately from recovery success. If repeated missions still show
a shared recovery defect, preserve this batch and isolate a new logical repair.

Source/install hashes are checked at each launch. No concurrent heavy build or
analysis during timed physics. Remote raw root /data/sun/vla_mppi_20261009/trials;
compact summary, per-trial CSV/JSON/logs, source/install and raw-retention hashes
