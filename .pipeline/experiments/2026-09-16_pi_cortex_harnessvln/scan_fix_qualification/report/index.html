Episode 3: why one check failed

At goal B the review needs 1.00 s of continuous stillness in the full 0.02 s physics record. One sample at sim 14.30 s had a yaw rate of 0.1595 rad/s (limit 0.15). The window before the arrival report was therefore 0.88 s. The backend's own dwell check polls a single overwritten snapshot file and never read that sample, so it reported arrival.

The runs used the intended V12 model_149999.pt (sha256 a9d31dce…). After a stop the runner keeps the RL policy running on a zero command and has no PD stance hold. As Dr Sun notes, an RL policy almost never stands perfectly still, and switching smoothly to a PD hold is hard. The failure sits in the locomotion stop, after SCAN; resubmission and arrival at A worked. It is counted as a failure, and no threshold was changed.
