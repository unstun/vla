The hold stands still

With the command at zero, V12 keeps stepping in place: yaw jitters around ±0.1 rad/s and the body creeps several centimetres. Once the PD hold owns the joints, the body stops. The 60 s stand compares the two directly.

Side view of the whole walk, real time. Top left is V12 alone; top right is the best of the earlier entries (damping stepped to 1.0); bottom row is the 1 s and 2 s crossfades. The strips below give forward speed and turn rate.

Getting into the hold without a kick

The entry was the hard part. V12's actuator applies position targets three physics substeps late but changes gains at once, so any gain step at the switch acts on a stale target.

  1. Gain step (C1). Kp 20 → 60 at once: the body hopped (0.21 m/s up, 0.30 rad/s yaw).
  2. Gain ramp over 0.5 s (C1b). The hop was gone, but yaw swung to 0.363 rad/s.
  3. Damping first (C1d). Three variants; each failed at one or more entries.
  4. Crossfade (D6), after a survey of published RL→PD switches. Keep V12's gains and live target at the switch, and blend its PD command into the hold command over 1 s, with Kd 1.0 from entry. Every entry stayed at or below 0.087 rad/s, the yaw already present on the entry row.

The hold does not reach the stop check

The review decides "stopped" from the 1 s before each terminal, and that second starts 0.10–0.38 s after the zero command. At that point V12 still drives the joints while the hold waits for its settle gates. All 24 still-window peaks fall in that settling phase, so the hold leaves the stop margin where it was. Restarts are a different story: with the hold, the yaw in the first second of walking is lower.

Episode 6 and the AC7 amendment

AC7 checks the first second after each hold entry for a kick. In episode 6 that second was cut short by a restart:

  1. At the cancel stop the hold waited 2.2 s for its support-balance gate, then entered at 6.66 s. The entry was calm (≤ 0.063 rad/s).
  2. 0.18 s later, at 6.84 s, the planner resubmitted goal A and the hold handed back to V12.
  3. V12's restart reached 0.174 rad/s at 6.92 s. The frozen rule counted that against the entry.

Dr Sun amended the window to end at the restart. That is the first row after entry in any mode other than STAND_TRANSITION or FIXED_STAND, and it is excluded. This was decided after the result and is labelled as such. AC8's walk check keeps its full 1 s window.
